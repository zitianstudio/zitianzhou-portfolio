User Flow

Facilitating a seamless onboarding experience for new users applying for Citi Pay through the merchant site is a key aspect of our design. Within the checkout process, users are afforded the option to select Citi Pay as their preferred payment method. Users transition to the application user flow, streamlining the process of applying for a Citi Pay Installment Loan. Subsequently, users are directed back to the merchant site to conclude their shopping journey, ensuring a cohesive and user-friendly transactional flow. Acquisition (ACQ) design approach prioritizes user convenience and coherence throughout the application and shopping experience.

1. Persona & Target Audience

My first task is to nail down the target audience and make personas based on user and merchant research.

  • Target population defined as Citi Shoppers Connection members who have used “Buy Now Pay Later“ (BNPL) services to finance purchases in the past (via Affirm, Afterpay, PayPal, etc.) and those who would be open to using Citi Pay.

    User Research Question >

  • Merchants are integrating Point-of-sale (POS) financing solutions to keep up with the competition and consumer demand as well as accessing a wider consumer base.

    Michael Kors shared perspective on need for physical POS capabilitie.

    Many merchants have other POS financing solutions integrated into the checkout experience and are looking for a differentiated value prop from Citi.

  • I crafted a persona named Joel, representing a officer facing the challenge of maintaining his lawn that requires weeding. However, budget constraints hinder his ability to invest in a lawnmower. This persona not only review his specific needs and pain points but also delves into his personal living habits and daily consumption preferences, providing a comprehensive understanding of his lifestyle and priorities.

    See other personas >

Leveraging the insights from the created persona, I designed a website specializing in the sale of lawnmowers. The user journey transitions to Citi Pay application process initiated through the checkout page of this website, marking the commencement of the user flow.

4. Qualification Check - Before ACQ

Let customers check if they pre-qualify for a Citi Pay before submitting an application that could impact their credit score.

User Pain Point: Uncertainty about whether they qualify for a loan, less stress about a check affecting their credit.

Solution:

1. For existed customer, system will check user’s information which is collected in Citi, also ask whether they want to edit.

2. For new customer, they only need to fill five information to pre-check: Home information, Monthly Housing Payment, Home Address, Employment Statues, and Annual Income.

Users will receive qualification pre-check notification before preparing to apply Citi Pay.

Users can choose whether they are already a Citi user.

If the user exists, their saved information will be automatically read, and users still can edit it. (Solution 1)

If they are not, they will need to fill out five informations. (Solution 2)

5. Verify Identity Design

After users submits application, system screens the details for some reasons such as fraud. If the user is flagged, the application will be put in a pending state and users will be alert that they need to call Citi to verifying their identity.

Once users calls Citi, the agent on the phone will update the application details and run it again to see if the applicant is approved or declined.

6. AutoPay in Citi Pay

We try to add autopay to our product to save customers’ time and effort to focus on other important aspects of their lives.

Based on autopay research, adding it to Citi Pay application user flow is in stone, which is at after users accept loan offer and before account set up.

  • To Users

    Convenience Process: AutoPay streamlines the loan payment process, removing the need for users to manually initiate payments. This efficient solution saves users valuable time and effort.

    Cost Saving: AutoPay helps user to avoid late fees and potential penalties for missed payment. Once user get penalty from Citi Pay, which will affect product’s brand favorability.

    Improved Credit Score: Consistently making on-time payment can have positive impact on user’s credit score. Benefit for user to secure better interest & terms for future loans.

    To Citi

    By offering AutoPay feature, Citi Pay can differentiate itself from competitors and provide a convenient, effective solution to their customer’s financial need.

    Increasing Retention: Convenient process of making payment can retain users. Also build user loyalty and repeat business.

    Reduce Operational Costs: AutoPay process can reduce operational costs that to associate with collections & recovery efforts. It could save significant cost over time.

  • We conducted extensive research on AutoPay within the realm of credit products, examining various approaches adopted by different banks and online financial institutions. Examples include American Express, Chase, Discover, as well as online financial platforms like Affirm and Sezzle, and Paypal Credit.

7. Celebratory Moment

After users complete the application process, I craft a celebratory message that signifies task completion. The celebratory design is not merely a visual element; it serves as an opportunity to strengthen the user's emotional connection with our product, leaving a lasting positive impression.

When I design those animations in Figma, I keep these things in mind.

  • Design in a clear and concise way that confirms successful completion of application user flow.

  • To align with Citi user flow’s style, don’t make it too fancy nor tedious.

  • Use friendly and encouraging language on user’s completion.

  • Make sure the celebratory elements don’t overwhelm the main massage.

8. Returned User Payment Design

Lack of incentives to use Citi Pay for purchases is a big problem in user test. So I designed user flow for Partner with merchants to offer cash back when customers use Citi Pay. 

I designed One-Click Signup allow customers to sign up for Citi Pay using their existing Citi account credentials with just one click.
