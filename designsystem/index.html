CRS Design System

Creating a Streamlined and Impactful Design System for Citi Retail Service (CRS): Ensuring Universal Applicability Across Retail Brands, Especially for Applying Citi Credit Cards.

What is CRS
Figma Prototype

ROLE

UI/UX Designer

TIMELINE

August 2022 - October 2022

TOOLS

Figma

PROJECT OVERVIEW

As a UX designer in the Citi Partnership Experience Design (PXD) team, my initial task involves crafting a versatile template for credit card applications. The design is universal, requiring only the adjustment of fonts to align with primary colors and brand identities. These credit cards are issued by various merchants in collaboration with Citi. My duty is to establish a robust design system for the application templates.

Ensure a unified visual language and consistent user interface elements throughout all components and interactions, promoting usability and a fluid user experience.

Decompose the design system into reusable components across diverse parts of the interface, fostering efficiency, reducing redundancy, and facilitating smoother updates for other design teams.

Collaborating closely with designers, project managers, content strategists, and developers, establish comprehensive documentation guidelines for the entire foundational system.

EXAMPLE SCREENS

COMPONENT GUIDE

Foster collaboration between designers, developers, and other stakeholders. Communication ensures that everyone is aligned with the design systems principles and guidelines in Figma.

We created a component guide with the aim of making it accessible to non-designers, providing clear instructions on how to review each buttons, fonts, dropdown menu, and ensuring pixel-to-pixel accuracy.
